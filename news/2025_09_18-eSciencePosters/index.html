UIC student authors presented several posters at eScience 2025, highlighting research in visualization, AI, and scientific workflows:

  • Interactive Exploration of HACC Cosmology Data using WebXR, Idunnuoluwa Adeniji, Joseph Insley, Michael Papka, Victor Mateevitsi, Silvio Rizzi, Janet Knowles, Mengjiao Han
  • Synthesis of Ground Truth for Neuronal Segmentation, Jyotsna Rajaraman, Thomas D. Uram, Kevin M. Boergens, Michael E. Papka
  • GENIUS: AI Powered Assistant for Scientific Research, Brian Ta, Ricky Massa, Aaqel Shaik, Mengjiao Han, Joseph Insley, Janet Knowles, Victor A. Mateevitsi, Michael E. Papka, Silvio Rizzi, Shilpika
  • Modular Agentic System for Scientific Visualization in Mixed Reality, Aaqel Shaik, Ricky Massa, Brian Ta, Mengjiao Han, Joseph Insley, Janet Knowles, Victor Mateevitsi, Michael Papka, Silvio Rizzi, Shilpika
