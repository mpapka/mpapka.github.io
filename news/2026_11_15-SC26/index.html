SC26 Comes to Chicago: Our Group has 10 Papers, 4 Posters, and an Art of HPC Piece

Created on November 15, 2026

2026

SC26 is at McCormick Place in Chicago this year, November 15–20, and we are excited to have the supercomputing community in our own backyard. It is a short trip for our UIC students and Argonne colleagues, and a chance to show off the city.

On Wednesday, November 18, at 7:30 pm, there is a special SC26 showing of Where Data Becomes Light: The Beauty of Scientific Visualization on ART on THE MART. The projection, a collaboration between Argonne and UIC’s Electronic Visualization Laboratory, covers the 2.5-acre river facade of THE MART and brings work from past SC Art of HPC exhibits and Scientific Visualization & Data Analytics Showcases to the city’s skyline.

Our group is part of 15 contributions to the program: a technical paper, nine workshop papers, four posters, and a piece in the Art of HPC exhibition.

The work runs from resilience, power, and scheduling on leadership-class systems to agentic user support and new ways of seeing large scientific data. Congratulations to the students presenting this week.
